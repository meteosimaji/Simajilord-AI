# Third-party licenses

Meteo Shogi AI directly depends on the following open-source packages. The package
distributions and linked upstream repositories contain their complete license texts.

| Package | Purpose | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| rsshogi | Shogi rules, board representation, and policy labels | MIT |
| MLX | Apple Silicon neural inference and training | MIT |
| NumPy | Numerical arrays and replay sampling | BSD-3-Clause |
| psutil | Unified-memory and process-pressure measurements | BSD-3-Clause |

External USI engines, evaluation functions, books, datasets, and teacher outputs are not
bundled dependencies. Their code, model, data, and output rights are reviewed separately in
[`MODEL_RIGHTS.md`](MODEL_RIGHTS.md), [`TEACHER_LINEAGE.md`](TEACHER_LINEAGE.md), and
[`DATASET_AUDIT.md`](DATASET_AUDIT.md).
